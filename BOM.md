# Flatbot BOM

Quantities come from the Onshape assembly (`cleanup` branch, "Flatbot Mechanical Assembly").
"Packs" means how many of the Amazon listing to buy; "Used" is how many pieces the model uses.

Two build configurations:

- **Caster cart**: 4 casters, no drive. Lets you roll the frame around before PACE drive modules arrive.
- **Powered**: 2 front casters, rear wheels driven by PACE RACER + ZLTech hub motors.

## Decks (laser cut)

Generated by the "Flatbot deck" FeatureScript feature in the **Decks** Part Studio. Export each plate as a DXF from Onshape.

| Part | Qty | Size (mm) | Notes |
|------|-----|-----------|-------|
| Deck Bottom - front | 1 | 580 × 421 × 6.35 | Notched around the posts and their braces. 10 × Ø8.5 mounting holes. |
| Deck Bottom - back | 1 | 580 × 379 × 6.35 | Notched around the posts and their braces. 10 × Ø8.5 mounting holes. |
| Deck Top - front | 1 | 580 × 421 × 6.35 | 11 × Ø8.5 mounting holes. |
| Deck Top - back | 1 | 580 × 379 × 6.35 | 11 × Ø8.5 mounting holes. |

Material: 1/4 in (6.35 mm) plywood, acrylic or HDPE. Each deck is split down the middle crossbar so a plate fits a 600 × 450 mm laser bed. To change frame length, width, profile, post length, middle-crossbar position, sheet thickness, hole size or spacing, edit the feature's parameters.
